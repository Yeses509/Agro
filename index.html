𒀭The ARQO Story

A bridge between memory and now.

ARQO begins with a simple idea: heritage can remain alive when it is approached with curiosity rather than nostalgia. The visual cultures of Mesopotamia offer an immense vocabulary—architecture, inscriptions, reliefs, plants, colors and everyday symbols.

These references are studied, distilled and reshaped into contemporary objects. The result is not a replica of the past, but a conversation with it: precise enough to honor its source and open enough to belong in the present.

The name

ARQO’s identity is built around continuity: a line from origin to object, from inherited memory to personal expression.

𒆠Materials & Care

Keep every piece close for longer.

Gold-plated jewelry

  • Keep away from perfume, lotions and household chemicals.
  • Remove before showering, swimming or exercising.
  • Wipe gently with a dry, soft cloth after wear.
  • Store separately in a dry pouch to reduce scratching.

Scarves and apparel

Follow the care instructions supplied with your piece. When in doubt, choose a gentle wash, cool water and air drying to preserve color and printed detail.
